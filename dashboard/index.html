How the model works

One formula scores every protocol. Nothing is hardcoded by name: the line is fitted from this snapshot's own live-token protocols at run time.

implied = exp(intercept + slope × log(TVL)) × growth × maturity × chains

  • Fitted line — ordinary least squares of log(market cap) on log(TVL) over the live-token protocols with a usable market cap (16 dead tokens with a recorded market cap of 0 or −1 are left out of the fit). The slope is 0.52 on this snapshot, so market cap grows with the square root of TVL: a flat multiple of TVL overprices the giants and underprices the small protocols. The category multiples below are the reference the fit is checked against, not the ranking rule.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) × 0.6.
  • Maturity — 0.6 + min(age, 1095 days) / 1095 × 0.4. Older with no token yet counts for more; unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Chains — 1 + 0.01 per extra chain, up to 10 chains.
  • Uncertainty range — an 80% conformal interval, calibrated on the pooled held-out residuals of the test below: the implied value times the 10th and 90th percentile of log(actual / predicted). The range is wide because market-cap-to-TVL varies a lot inside a category; the dashboard shows it on every row's detail view.
  • Launch odds — the share of protocols in the same category that already have a token, smoothed, discounted by age. A base rate from the peers' record, not a prediction of whether or when a token is issued.
  • Farm verdict — the plan's cost against its expected reward. Cost is gas for six steps on the protocol's first chain (Ethereum $5, BSC $0.10, Solana $0.05, other chains $0.50 a step), impermanent loss or slippage on the capital (2% for liquidity positions, 0.5% otherwise), and the opportunity cost of locking the capital at 4% a year. Expected reward is the launch odds times 5% of the implied value — the stated airdrop pool — shared among the estimated farmers (TVL / $5,000 each, at least 100). “Worth farming” clears the cost, “Marginal” is positive but below it, “Skip” is negative. Every assumption is stated here so the verdict can be re-derived from the row's own figures.

Held-out test — the model is tested, not just asserted

The live protocols are split in two deterministic ways and the full model is fitted on one half and asked to predict the other half's market caps. The baseline is the radar-3 model — each category's median multiple with the same growth, maturity and chain terms — scored on the same splits, so the fit's gain is measured, not claimed.

Growth, maturity and chains are kept because each one was tested on both splits and only the terms that helped both stayed; the category-intercept and multi-variable variants were tried and dropped for the same reason.

What “pre-token” means

A protocol is listed as pre-token when its DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. That is what the record says — never proof it has no token. A protocol on the list may still have a token its record does not carry. The board never predicts whether or when a token is issued; the implied value is this entry's own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is.

The board ranks all 758 protocols, not only the candidates: a protocol whose token already trades is ranked by its market cap, a protocol whose token trades but the snapshot carries no market cap for is ranked by the model's implied value and marked live-unpriced, and a pre-token protocol that is not a launch candidate is ranked the same way and marked with the reason. The board leaves out wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade as candidates — the same exclusion the snapshot's own watchlist rule uses (Bridge, Liquid Staking and related categories, names starting with an exchange's name, names carrying “wrapped”, “staked” or “staking”, and versioned pools such as “V2”). A launch candidate has to be a protocol of its own, not a wrapper around someone else's token. Those rows are listed on the Not candidates tab with the reason.
